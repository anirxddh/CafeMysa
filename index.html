About Us!

Cafe Mysa is a space made for slowing down. It’s the kind of place where mornings begin unhurried, conversations flow easily, and time feels a little less demanding. We focus on simple comforts — good food, carefully brewed coffee, and an atmosphere that signals you can stay as long as you like. Whether you’re stopping by for a quiet meal, working through the afternoon, or catching up with someone you care about, Mysa is meant to feel familiar and welcoming.

Our menu brings together continental favorites and freshly brewed coffee, prepared with care and consistency. But what truly defines Cafe Mysa is the feeling it creates. From students and working professionals to couples and regulars who return for their favorite corner, Mysa has grown into a place where people come not just to eat, but to feel at ease. Every detail, from the space to the service, is shaped around making each visit comfortable, relaxed, and quietly memorable.
